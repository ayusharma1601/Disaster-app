  • National Emergency Response Helpline: 112
  • Assam State Disaster Management Helpline: 1070

1. Scope and Purpose of Service

The Disaster Intelligence Platform aggregates multi-agency telemetry, automated weather station (AWS) rainfall readings, Central Water Commission (CWC) river gauges, and community situational reports for the Dhemaji flood basin. The information provided is strictly for situational awareness and operational coordination.

2. Statutory Precedence of Official Government Bulletins

Official alerts, flood forecasts, and evacuation directives issued by the India Meteorological Department (IMD), the Central Water Commission (CWC), and the Assam State Disaster Management Authority (ASDMA) take absolute precedence over any heuristic risk indicators or analog trend predictions generated by this software.

3. Community Reporting & Data Privacy Policy

  • Zero PII Exposure: Reporter phone numbers and confidential contact details are strictly encrypted or stripped from public endpoints and never displayed on public feeds or client interfaces.
  • Unverified Status: All citizen-submitted incident reports enter the database in an UNVERIFIED state and remain labeled as such until verified by a designated emergency operations coordinator.
  • Prohibition of False or Panic-Inducing Reports: Submitting malicious, fictitious, or panic-inducing hazard reports is strictly prohibited under the Disaster Management Act, 2005.

4. Shelter Capacity & Freshness Disclaimer

In accordance with the 24-hour Shelter Freshness Protocol, relief camp capacities that have not been physically verified by local revenue circle officers within 24 hours are marked as UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED CAPACITY. Responders and citizens must verify access road safety before attempting travel to relief centers.

5. Cyber Security & Fair Use Policy

Automated scraping, denial-of-service attempts, cross-site script injections, and unauthorized attempts to access responder authentication tokens are strictly blocked by IP-based rate limiting and security filtering. Violations will be logged in the immutable audit ledger and reported to competent authorities.
